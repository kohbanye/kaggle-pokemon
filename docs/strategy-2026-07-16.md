# Path to Top-100 (Elo 1000+) — Strategy Review, 2026-07-16

Investigation of the live leaderboard, top public agents, and the metagame, with a
concrete go-forward plan. Written after reading the #1 (Starmie, 1310), the Archaludon
counter, the official RL/MCTS sample, the 5th-place Alakazam, and the July-8 meta data.

## 0. Where we stand (hard numbers)

- Our team `kohbanye`: **rank 3037 / 5096, score 615.3** (best submission ≈ g8_e0 622).
- **Top-100 cutoff ≈ 991.5.** Only 48 teams ≥1000, 8 ≥1100, 1 ≥1300 (`Majkel1337` 1310).
- The gap 615 → 1000 is **~385 Elo = structural, not ladder noise** (noise is ±200).
- Scoring quirk: **only the 2 most recent submissions count and rating resets per
  version** — every top team shows SubmissionCount = 2. Swapping decks/agents is
  *zero-risk*; there is no reason to sit on a 615 agent.

## 1. Why the top agents win (evidence, not theory)

Every agent at 1000+ that we could inspect is **rule-based, piloting a real
tournament netdeck, with exact lethal forward-search**. None is deep RL.

- **#1 Starmie/Froslass (1310), masamikobayashi** — copied a real Limitless list
  (ashleysandlin). Architecture: *Generic mode* (priority-scoring) + *matchup modes*
  (Lucario/Iono/Crustle) + *Finish mode* = **`search_step` lethal verification**
  ("Can I win this turn?"), replay the verified line. Plus a **PrizeTracker**
  (published, ~140 LOC) so the search never imagines a prized card. Motto:
  *"Normal turns handled by rules; winning turns verified by search."*
- **5th Alakazam ex, ryotasueyoshi** — pure rule-based. Chose the deck *"because it's
  easy to handle on rule-based logic"* — damage is a **closed form `20 × hand size`**, so
  a hand-size optimizer plays it near-optimally. Discipline: never over-draw past lethal,
  never attach redundant energy, deck-out safety gating, lethal→max-prize→chip target ladder.
- **romanrozen LB~950** — matchup-aware priority scorer on an **Archaludon Metal-tempo**
  deck; ~950 Elo comes from *setup-before-attack ordering + Crustle overrides + anti-mill
  refill*, no net.
- **Official RL+MCTS sample (kiyotah, 795 votes)** — a **scaffold, not a contender**:
  1-layer Transformer, **10 sims/move, 5 self-play generations, opponent belief = all
  Snorlax + basic energy junk**, toy deck. Makes **no claim of beating heuristics**. Its
  real gift is the feature-encoding template and the author's line: *"the Search API is
  useful for rule-based agents too"* — i.e. use `search_step` to resolve effect
  uncertainty, not to run deep search.

**Takeaway:** the engine exposes **exact forward simulation** (`search_begin/step/end`),
so the bulk of play-Elo (lethal lines, damage checks) is available to a *heuristic* for
free. That is why hand-rules + verified search beat learned play here.

## 2. Why *we* are at 615 (the smoking gun)

Two independent, fixable defects — and neither is "our RL isn't good enough."

1. **Our deck is not a deck.** `g8_e0` (our "best") is a QD/MAP-Elites Frankenstein:
   **five energy types (G6/M3/F2/P2/D2), a 1-of Dragapult ex, a 2-of Crustle line, and a
   pile of singleton trainers.** It has no coherent game plan. It scores OK in a local sim
   that is *known to mispredict the ladder*, and it collapses on the real ladder. Winners'
   decks are **mono-energy and consistent** (Archaludon: 4/4/4 + 11 Metal; Alakazam:
   4/4/3 mono-Psychic). **This alone likely explains most of the 385-Elo gap.**
2. **Our submitted pilot is a 96-line greedy bot** — no lethal search, no prize tracking,
   no matchup detection, no setup-before-attack scoring. The four things every 1000+ agent
   has, we have zero of, in the bundle we actually submit.

All of our sophisticated machinery (AZ-LSTM, ISMCTS, ReBeL, QD) is **local research that
never reached a good submission**, and it targets **play (the smaller lever)** while the
**deck (the bigger lever)** is left to produce incoherent card-salad.

Grimmsnarl-Dark, Starmie, Cynthia's Garchomp. Must respect Crustle (bring a counter or
hard-code anti-mill play). Recommended: a **2-deck portfolio with decorrelated matchups**
across the 2 submission slots.

## 4. Honest read on "RL-based strong agent"

The user's goal is an RL-based agent. The evidence — the whole top of the ladder, the
official sample's own framing, and **our own repeated negative results** (AZ/ISMCTS/ReBeL
never beat `greedy_plus`; see memory) — says an **end-to-end deep-RL *play* agent will not
reach top-100 by 2026-08-16**, because:

- Exact `search_step` gives a heuristic the tactical Elo for free.
- Imperfect-info belief is hard; determinized search fills junk → our ISMCTS lost on the
  real ladder.
- The local sim mispredicts the ladder → RL overfits the wrong target.
- Compute is tiny (CPU, offline, seconds/move) — no room for big search or big nets.
- **RL doesn't touch deck coherence, which is the dominant lever.**

This does **not** mean abandon learning. It means put learning **where it actually adds
value the heuristic can't**, on top of the proven backbone — a *hybrid*, which is also
exactly what the **Strategy track ($240k, report-scored, deadline 9/13)** rewards.

## 5. Plan

### Verification result (2026-07-16, native local) — reshapes Phase A

- Built the published **Archaludon netdeck** (`decklists/archaludon_netdeck.csv`,
  engine-legal). Piloted by generic **greedy_plus it scored only 0.32 vs greedy_plus|g8_e0**
  — because Cinderace is a **Stage-2 with the special "Explosiveness" skill** and the
  Duraludon→Archaludon combo needs bespoke timing greedy_plus doesn't know. **A strong
  netdeck with a generic pilot bricks.** Top decks are *inseparable from their pilots*.
- Our current `greedy|g8_e0` vs the real-meta heldout2 pool (opp=greedy_plus) averages
  **~0.69** yet the deck is **615 live** — because the local *opponents* are also
  generic-piloted, so their evolution/combo decks under-develop and simple greedy-pilotable
  aggro is **systematically overrated locally**. This is the exact mechanism of the
  local↔ladder mismatch. **Stop trusting generic-pilot local win-rates.**

**Implication:** Phase A is not "netdeck + generic pilot." It is **(coherent deck + its
bespoke rule pilot) as a unit**, plus a deck-agnostic pilot backbone (lethal search,
PrizeTracker, setup≫attack) shared across archetypes.

### Phase A — bank a top-100-adjacent score now (days, low risk)

The proven recipe. Do this regardless of the RL work; the free slots make it costless.

1. **Netdeck a coherent high-score archetype.** Start with **Cynthia's Garchomp ex**
   (56.9%, beats Crustle) and/or **Starmie** (57.2%). Reconstruct the real Limitless list
   in `decklists/` as mono-energy 4/4/x lines. **Stop submitting QD card-salad.**
2. **Build a real rule-based pilot** to replace the 96-line greedy bot, with the four
   proven components:
   - priority-scoring, **setup ≫ attack** (attack ranked last because it ends the turn);
   - **`detect_matchup()` + overrides** (esp. Crustle wall/mill: switch off 0-damage
     attacks, anti-mill deck refill);
   - **lethal forward-search** via `search_begin/step/end` ("can I win this turn?"),
     replay the verified line;
   - **PrizeTracker** (port the published ~140-LOC class) so the search stays honest.
3. **Portfolio the 2 slots** with two decorrelated decks; submit, then read the drift over
   hours (not the first number). Target: cross 900, then 1000.

### Phase B — the learning arm, done so it actually helps (the real project)

Not end-to-end play-RL. Targeted learning bolted onto the Phase-A backbone:

- **B0 — Fix the eval loop first (the meta-bug).** Rebuild the opponent pool from the
  **real meta netdecks** (top ~10 archetypes; we already have `heldout2/` + `lossdecks/`),
  and gate everything on *that*, not the free-form local sim. Every past failure traces
  back to optimizing a mispredicting target.
- **B1 — Learned value net as a leaf evaluator *inside* the exact search.** Keep lethal
  search exact; use a small net only to evaluate **non-lethal strategic** positions for a
  few plies of `search_step`. This is AlphaZero-for-imperfect-info done pragmatically
  (rules + verified search + learned strategic depth) — the one place a net beats the
  heuristic. Uses the LSTM `RecurrentPolicyValueNet` we already have.
- **B2 — Optimize the heuristic weights with population-based search / CMA-ES / bandits**
  against the B0 meta pool. The policy *is* the parameterized scorer; reward = win-rate vs
  meta. (The Archaludon author hand-grid-searched heal thresholds — automate and generalize
  that across all weights.) This is the highest-ROI "RL-flavored" lever and can't degrade
  below the tuned baseline.
- **B3 — Supervised opponent/deck classifier** from replays → selects the matchup mode and
  seeds the search belief (fixes the junk-belief failure that sank ISMCTS).
- **B4 — Deck search over *coherent* decks only.** If QD is kept, constrain the encoding to
  mono/dual-energy consistent lines and optimize over the B0 real-meta pool — never
  free-form card-salad again. Or simply curate 4–6 netdecks and let B2/B3 pick.

### Sequencing & guardrails

- Do **A now** (this week) — it is the fastest Elo and de-risks the deadline.
- Then **B0 → B2/B3 → B1**. B1 (learned search value) is the most research-y and the
  place the RL ambition lives; it only pays off after B0 makes eval trustworthy.
- **Gate on real-meta win-rate with CIs**, never a single ladder read. Validate big moves
  live (2 free slots).
- The hybrid (rules + verified search + learned strategic eval + PBT-tuned weights +
  meta-coverage deck) is a clean story for the **Strategy track** too.
